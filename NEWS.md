# Changelog

## Unreleased

To be released with the next batch of trials.

- The two NextGen Cassava tricot trials in Uganda join the release, 240 sites in 2019 to 2020 and 384 in 2021 to 2022, with 20 elite clones and the check NAROCASS 1. The data were co-produced by the National Crops Resources Research Institute of NARO and Cornell University, with Robert Sezi Kawuki as principal investigator and Hale Tufan as project leader, under NextGen Cassava (INV-007637, Gates Foundation and FCDO). Nanyonjo et al. (2024) describe both, and the clones carry their source, pedigree and role from that paper. The release now holds 505 trials and 90,363 sites across 41 crops in 18 countries, from 20 data producers.
- Irene Bayiyana, Williams Esuma, Michael Kanaabi, Nicholas Muhumuza, Ephraim Nuwamanya, Alfred Ozimati and Enoch Wembabazi join the author list of the paper, `CITATION.cff` and `.zenodo.json`, which now hold 208 authors. Ann Ritah Nanyonjo and Robert Sezi Kawuki are written as in the paper.
- Both Uganda sweetpotato consumer tests credit the same team, Thiago Mendes (principal investigator), Reuben Ssali (project manager), and Jacob van Etten, Benard Yada and Florence Osaru of the National Agricultural Research Organisation (NARO) as project members, with NARO as partner. Florence Osaru joins the author list of the paper, `CITATION.cff` and `.zenodo.json` (201 authors), and Benard Yada is listed with NARO.
- The sweetpotato consumer test SPGSP22 of CIP in Uganda (2022, 240 consumers, nine genotypes) joins the release, under SweetGAINS (Gates Foundation), with Thiago Mendes as principal investigator, Reuben Ssali as project manager, and Jacob van Etten, Benard Yada and Florence Osaru as project members.
- The Uganda consumer test of boiled sweetpotato of the same study (Moyo et al. 2021, 418 participants, 2019) joins the release.
- The 29 sweetpotato trials of the International Potato Center (CIP) in Ghana join the release, with CSIR-CRI and CSIR-SARI as partners. They are 27 farmer-managed trials of 2020 and 2021 under the RTB Scaling Fund project and SweetGAINS, led by Edward Carey and Thiago Mendes, and the two consumer tests of boiled sweetpotato of 2019 described by Moyo et al. (2021). Variety names follow their release names (local names Obare and Tu-Purple in remarks). The 2020 trials carry planting dates read from the farmers' answers, and a planting window where none was given (`planting_date_source`).
- Joseph Adjebeng-Danquah, Daniel Akansake, Eric K. Dery, Hannele Lindqvist-Kreuze, Mariam Nakitto and Sam Namanda join the author list of the paper, `CITATION.cff` and `.zenodo.json`, which now hold 200 authors.
- Two sweetpotato trials of the International Potato Center (CIP) in Mozambique join the release (2024, 145 sites, orange-fleshed and white-fleshed clones), under the Root, Tuber, and Banana (RTB) Breeding Network funded by the Gates Foundation, with the Instituto de Investigação Agrária de Moçambique (IIAM) as partner. Thiago Mendes leads them. Sweetpotato and Mozambique are new to the release.
- Gervancio Covele, Bert de Boeck and Gonzaga Madroba join the author list of the paper, `CITATION.cff` and `.zenodo.json`.
- The two on-farm maize trials of the Seed Production Technology for Africa (SPTA) Phase II project of CIMMYT in Kenya join the release, 358 sites in 2022 and 1,379 in 2023. Jill E. Cairns leads both, with Rachel C. Voss (2022) and Michael Ndegwa (2023) as project managers and George M. Kanyenji as project member. Voss et al. (2023) describe the 2022 trial and Dorado-Betancourt et al. (2026) analyse both. The files list seven grants of the Gates Foundation, FFAR and USAID.
- Michael Ndegwa and Rachel C. Voss join the author list of the paper, `CITATION.cff` and `.zenodo.json`.
- Four sorghum trials of Maseno University in Homabay and Migori counties, Kenya, join the release (2021 and 2022, 879 sites, 12 varieties), led by Evans Ochieng Ouma.
- Mainassara Zaman-Allah leaves the author list of the paper, `CITATION.cff` and `.zenodo.json`. His Zimbabwe maize project of 2023 held registration data only and leaves the dataset the paper describes, which now counts 600 trials, 553 of them farmer-managed, with 102,539 tricot sites in 22 countries. The cumulative sites figure is redrawn.
- The banana trial of the Centre de coopération internationale en recherche agronomique pour le développement (CIRAD) in Guadeloupe joins the release (RéVAI project, 28 farms, four CIRAD dessert banana hybrids, 2023 to 2024). Banana and Guadeloupe are new to the release. The file credits Lucile Toniutti, Coline Tourreille, Thierry Van Kin Tran and Charlotte Chemarin, and the thesis of Tourreille (2024, Norwegian University of Life Sciences) describes the trial.
- CIRAD is named by its full French name in every file, with ROR https://ror.org/05kpkpg04. The cassava concept test of Colombia carries it as partner.
- Lucile Toniutti, Coline Tourreille and Charlotte Chemarin (CIRAD), Geon Kang (Scuola Superiore Sant'Anna) and Evans Ochieng Ouma (Maseno University) join the author list of the paper, `CITATION.cff` and `.zenodo.json`.
- `docs/trial-locations.png` and `docs/trial-xy.csv` show the 505 trials of the release, including the four concept tests of CIMMYT market intelligence and the cassava concept test of Colombia. The crop group "Roots and tubers" becomes "Roots, tubers and bananas". The paper map `docs/trial-locations-global.png` is the full world map in the Equal Earth projection, no longer cropped, and shows the banana sites.
- The video-based product concept test of cassava in the Caribbean region of Colombia joins the release (CIAT and CIRAD, 398 farmers in Bolívar, Córdoba and Sucre, November and December 2024, seven concepts), from the public record at Harvard Dataverse, https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/R57LRD. Cassava and Colombia are new to the release, which now holds 463 trials and 82,583 sites across 39 crops in 16 countries. The file holds the department and municipality, the kind of cassava grown and its area, the concept the farmer would plant, the reasons for the choices translated from Spanish to English, and the farm or interview point rounded to two decimals.
- Four video-based product concept tests of CIMMYT market intelligence join the release, which now holds 462 trials and 82,185 sites in 15 countries, from 17 data producers. They are maize in Kenya and Uganda (1,200 participants each, 2022), groundnut in Tanzania (4,789) and sorghum in Tanzania (1,100). Each participant ranked three product concepts shown in videos. The items are product concepts, not varieties, and `type` is "concept testing".
- The two maize concept tests hold the household survey of the study, 30 variables on the household, its land, crops, income, seed and the use of the last harvest, with county or district, sub-county or county, the interview point rounded to two decimals and the interview date. Grain quantities are in kg, converted from 90 kg bags in Kenya and 100 kg bags in Uganda.
- The two maize concept tests are the first files under CC BY 4.0, the licence of their source. Every other file stays CC0 1.0. `datasets.csv` and `metadata.license` say which applies, and the schema accepts both.
- The parquet tables do not include the four files yet and follow with the next build.
- 27 trials from Tanzania join the release, which now holds 458 trials and 73,896 sites. They are 25 common bean trials of the International Center for Tropical Agriculture (CIAT) and the Tanzania Agricultural Research Institute (TARI) run from 2021 to 2024, the common bean trial of One Acre Fund of 2021, and the maize and common bean intercrop trial of TARI of 2022, filed under maize. The CIAT and TARI trials belong to the Pan-Africa Bean Research Alliance (PABRA) and were funded by AVISA and 1000FARMS. Each file names its project leader, project managers and members.
- Clare M Mukankusi and Jean Claude Rubyogo of CIAT join the author list of the paper, `CITATION.cff` and `.zenodo.json`, which now hold 185 authors. Fadhili Kasubiri is listed as Fadhili Salum Kasubiri.
- `docs/trial-locations.png` and `docs/trial-xy.csv` show the 458 trials.
- Each release carries every trial in three parquet tables, `plots`, `sites` and `variables`, built from the trial files by `script/build-parquet.py` (Brayden Youngberg) and attached to the release. They follow schema v3.0 column for column, with `trial_id` added. The JSON files stay the full record.
- All files move to release schema v3.0, which adopts the standardization that Robert J. Hijmans built and reviewed for this dataset. The 431 trials and 70,650 sites are the same.
- Traits, site variables and collection moments have one standard name per concept across trials. `trait_original` keeps the question code of the trial, `metadata.variables[].original_name` the earlier name of a site variable, and `metadata.study.collection_moments` gains `standard_moment`, `visit` and `assessor`. The site columns `country_name`, `crop_name`, `coordinate_source`, `coordinate_uncertainty_m` and `coordinate_flag` are now `country`, `crop`, `geo_source`, `geo_uncertainty` and `geo_flag`.
- Measured values gain `value_standard` and `unit_standard`, the value in one unit per trait. `value` and `unit` stay as recorded. `yield_plot` is the weight harvested from the plot and `yield` the yield per area in kg/ha.
- Yields derived from a plot weight carry the flag `derived`, and `plot_area_assumed` as well where the plot area is the tricot protocol size and not a reported one. A plot area stated in a publication replaces the protocol size in two groundnut trials of Tanzania. Central America common bean has its protocol plot of 12 m2.
- Administrative units sit in `adm1` to `adm5` as defined for the country, with the village in `location`. Names follow GADM 4.1 where they match. `metadata.admin_levels` says what each level is in the country.
- Categorical answers of the site table have one spelling across trials, in English.
- 13 site dates that were clear typing errors are repaired, by a rule that changes a date only when the other sites of the trial show what it should be.
- The README and `SKILL.md` say how to treat the 16 farmer-managed trials with fewer than 15 sites, which stay in the release.
- Joachim Madeni of the Tanzania Agricultural Research Institute, who leads the groundnut programme, joins the author list of the paper, `CITATION.cff` and `.zenodo.json`.
- The five groundnut trials of Tanzania and the six cowpea trials of IITA in Nigeria name their programme as the Dryland Crops Program (DCP, formerly AVISA) and 1000FARMS, in place of the Africa Dryland Crops Improvement Network (ADCIN). Variety details follow the variety catalogue of groundnuts in Tanzania, with release year 2018 for Naliendele 2016 and Tanzanut 2016 and the original ICRISAT codes in the remarks.
- The release grows from 64 to 431 trials and from 18,570 to 70,650 sites, across 38 crops in 14 countries. New are 114 trials from India (Seeds for Needs, the UNEP-GEF project and the ICAR-Bioversity workplan trials of 2025, with 34 mother trials of participatory varietal selection), 220 from Ethiopia (ISSD Ethiopia and the Seeds for Needs durum wheat trials), the bean trials of Central America, three cowpea trials from Ghana and four pearl millet trials from Nigeria. The Laos forage trial gains its entry descriptions, authors and funders.
- All files move to release schema v2.1. Site coordinates are rounded to two decimals instead of displaced at random, so a published point lies within about 800 m of the recorded one and repeated downloads give the same point. `plot_data` gains `moment_date`, the date a collection moment took place at the site, and `moment_days` is defined as the planned day of the moment, counted from the start of the trial. These changes follow the review by Robert J. Hijmans.
- Researcher-managed companion trials are new in the release. Their files carry `pvs` in the name, and `metadata.companions` and `metadata.companion_of` link them to the tricot trials on the same genotypes.
- Producers, project leaders, members and funders were confirmed with the partners for the India, Ethiopia, Central America, Nigeria and Laos trials.
- `docs/trial-locations.png` maps the trials in this repository and `docs/trial-locations-global.png` all trials described in the data paper. Both use the Equal Earth projection. The cropped paper map is removed.
- The data paper draft has the current map, counts and author list, 183 authors with one headquarters address per institution.
- `CITATION.cff` and `.zenodo.json` carry the same 183 authors. Both are now written by one script from `metadata/project-metadata.xlsx` and are updated with every batch.
- The data paper counts drop to 599 trial files, 552 tricot trials and 101,811 sites. Two groundnut trials of Tanzania 2021 (groundnut2 and OPP1114827) were copies of gnut21TZ, the same 154 farms under coded variety names, and are removed from the processing repository. Neither was in this repository, so no published file changes.
- The 64 trials of the first release first moved to release schema v2.0, built in the data managers' processing repository. No trial or site was added, and the files cover the same 18,570 sites.
- Files now live at `data/<crop>/<crop>-<iso3>-<trial_id>.json`, one folder per crop. `datasets.csv` gives the `path` of each file, keyed by `trial_id`.
- `block_data` is renamed `site_data` and gains country, crop and administrative names (region, district, village) where recorded. `plot_data` gains `moment_days`, harmonised trait names and ids, `trait_original`, `unit` and `flag`. `metadata.variables` now gives the harmonised question id, Crop Ontology term, value type and unit of every column.
- The wheat trial is now filed under `breadwheat`, matching the crop list used across the dataset.
- `rank_analysis` is removed. The files hold observations only, and model results are left to the analyses that use them. The rankings are unchanged in `plot_data`.
- `metadata/tricot-trial.schema.json` and `metadata/data-dictionary.csv` describe the release schema, including the optional `genotype_data` table and the companion links. All files validate.
- License changed from CC BY-SA 4.0 to CC0 1.0 (public domain). The export scripts now write the CC0 license into each trial file.
- The dataset index moved from `data/aa-available-datasets.csv` to `datasets.csv`. It is rebuilt from the trial files by `script/helper/helper-05-build-dataset-index.R`, uses ISO dates, and adds end date, objective, number of blocks and genotypes, collection moments, ranked traits, file size and license.
- New `metadata/tricot-trial.schema.json` and `metadata/data-dictionary.csv` document the trial files, and `script/helper/helper-06-validate-trial-files.R` checks them.
- Trial files now carry the concept DOI, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17112491, which always resolves to the latest version.
- `.zenodo.json` is now written in Zenodo's deposit format, so Zenodo reads it on release.
- Added `SKILL.md`, `AGENTS.md` and `llms.txt`, aligning the repository with the AI-ready documentation for tricot. They guide users and AI assistants on using the data, which are read-only.
- Added `MAINTAINERS.md`, the guide for the tricot data managers, and an issue template for reporting data errors.
