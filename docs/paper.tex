  \begin{abstract}
  We present a harmonized global dataset generated through the tricot
  (triadic comparisons of technology options) citizen science approach,
  comprising decentralized on-farm agricultural experiments conducted
  between 2011 and 2025 across more than 25 countries and over 30 crop
  species. The dataset represents the outcome of a long-term effort to
  develop a FAIR-by-design infrastructure for agricultural
  experimentation, integrating standardized protocols, digital data
  collection, controlled vocabularies, environmental covariates,
  socio-economic information, and links to external repositories. The
  resulting resource provides a unique foundation for studying farmer

\section{Introduction}\label{introduction}

Agricultural innovation increasingly requires decentralized,
context-specific evidence generated directly in the target environments
\citep{deSousa2021, Atlin2001}. The tricot (triadic comparison of
technology options) approach was developed to address this challenge by
enabling large-scale citizen science experimentation in agriculture
\citep{vanEtten2016, vanEtten2011}. Early implementations in Ethiopia,
India, and Central America demonstrated that farmers could generate
robust experimental data through simple, distributed trial designs
\citep{vanEtten2016, vanEtten2019, deSousa2021, deSousa2024}.

\section{The tricot research
infrastructure}\label{the-tricot-research-infrastructure}

Over the last fifteen years, tricot evolved from a participatory
experimental method into a global digital FAIR-by-design infrastructure
supporting decentralized agricultural experimentation.

Short description of the development phases, for example Phase I
(2011--2016) where we tested tricot as proof of concept with pilot
trials in Ethiopia, India, Central America \citep{vanEtten2016}, early
digital tools \citep{vanEtten2016} and initial climate adaptation
applications \citep{vanEtten2019}.

Then Phase II (2017--2022) on the digitalization and scaling, with the
new version of ClimMob \citep{Quiros2024}, expansion across countries,
crops and contexts

and integration into breeding programs
\citep{Daudi2026, Sharma2025, Kang2025, EmmanuelAlamu2023}.

Phase III (2022--Present), standardized metadata, ontology mapping, SOP
